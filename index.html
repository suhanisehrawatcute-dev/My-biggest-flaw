A not very long time ago, in a galaxy that is ours, a girl wrote a poem...
My Biggest
Flaw
I want magic. I don't mean that I want wands or silly staffs. I want to live through an adventure every day. I want to take risks and travel the world with my friends. I want to find out if the villain is what they seem. I want to see through the riddles of the cunning prince. I want to wake up with the sun and sleep under the stars. I want to get advice from a little green guy. I want to fly in my spaceship and escape death. I want to confront someone who is more powerful than me and lose. I want to meet someone who still has goodness in their evil. I want to adventure like no one before. I don't want to live the mundane life. With a 9 to 5, a school, a job, a routine. I want magic.
